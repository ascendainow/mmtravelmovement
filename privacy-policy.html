Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 22, 2026

Michaux Melodic Travel Movement ("MMTM," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit mmtravelmovement.com or contact us regarding travel planning services.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, book a consultation, or contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, destination preferences, travel dates, budget range, number of travelers, type of travel, and any notes or special requests you provide. If you schedule a consultation through our Calendly link, Calendly's own privacy practices also apply to that booking. We do not knowingly collect payment card or banking details through our website; any payments are handled directly with Melody Michaux or third-party suppliers (airlines, hotels, cruise lines, tour operators) through their own secure payment channels.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, prepare travel proposals and itineraries, coordinate bookings with third-party travel suppliers, and communicate with you about your trip. We may also use your contact information to follow up about your travel plans or share relevant travel offers, and you may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

Sharing of Information

We share information with third-party suppliers (such as airlines, hotels, cruise lines, and tour operators, including our affiliated network Jus Adventures) only as needed to research, quote, or book your travel arrangements. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand site traffic and improve the user experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at melody@mmtravelmovement.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to melody@mmtravelmovement.com or 470-246-8349.
